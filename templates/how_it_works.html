How It Works

From Zero to Your First WhatsApp Campaign

No developer, no API docs to read. Here's everything that happens between signing up and your first message going out.

Step 1

Connect your WhatsApp Business Account

Click "Connect WhatsApp" on your profile and Meta's official Embedded Signup pops up — log in with the Facebook account that manages your WhatsApp Business number, pick your business, and you're connected. No API keys, no app review, no waiting on Meta's support queue.

Step 2

Bring in your contacts

Upload your contact list as a spreadsheet, or add people one at a time. Organize them with tags — by city, customer type, or whatever makes sense for your business — so you can target exactly who should get a given campaign. Anyone who opts out is automatically excluded from every future send.

Step 3

Create your message template

Write your message with text, an image, video, or document header, and buttons — then submit it for Meta's approval straight from the dashboard. Every template's status (approved, pending, rejected) stays in sync automatically, so you always know what's ready to send.

Step 4

Launch your campaign and watch it in real time

Pick your audience — everyone, a tag, or a hand-picked list — send instantly or schedule for later, and watch delivered, read, clicked and replied counts update live. If something fails, you'll see exactly why (invalid number, opted out, outside the messaging window).

Step 5

Follow up and automate what comes next

Filter any campaign by status — delivered-but-unread, clicked-but-silent, failed — and relaunch a follow-up to just that group in a couple of clicks. Or build a Flow once: greet new contacts, answer common questions, and hand off to support automatically, with no code involved.
