We're Making WhatsApp Marketing Simple

Dovix AI helps businesses turn their WhatsApp Business Account into a real marketing and support channel — without writing a line of code or hiring a developer to wrangle Meta's API.

Our Story

Built because setting up WhatsApp for business shouldn't take a dev team.

The official WhatsApp Business Platform (Cloud API) is powerful, but it's built for engineers — raw JSON payloads, template approval rules, 24-hour messaging windows, and webhook plumbing. Most businesses just want to message their customers and see what happened after. Dovix AI was built to close that gap: connect your WhatsApp Business Account in a few clicks through Meta's official Embedded Signup, and get a complete marketing tool on top of it — campaigns, templates, contacts, automation, and analytics, all in one dashboard.
