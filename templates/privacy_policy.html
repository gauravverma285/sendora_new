Privacy Policy

Last updated: {% now "F j, Y" %}

Dovix AI ("we", "us", "our") provides a WhatsApp Business messaging platform that lets businesses connect their own WhatsApp Business Account (WABA), create message templates, manage contacts, and run messaging campaigns through the Meta WhatsApp Business Platform (Cloud API). This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and how it is used.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: username, email address, and password when you register.
  • WhatsApp Business connection data: when you connect your WhatsApp Business Account through Facebook Login / WhatsApp Embedded Signup, we receive and store your WhatsApp Business Account ID (WABA ID), phone number ID, phone number, Meta Business ID, and an access token that lets our platform send messages and manage templates on your behalf.
  • Contacts you upload: names, phone numbers, and any tags or attributes you add for the audiences you message through our platform.
  • Campaign and template data: message templates you create, campaign configurations, chat messages sent and received through our Messages inbox, and delivery/read/engagement metrics returned by the WhatsApp Business Platform.
  • Billing information: the subscription plan you choose and your payment/subscription history. We do not store card details ourselves.
  • Usage data: basic technical data such as IP address and browser type, collected for security and troubleshooting.

2. How We Use Information

  • To operate your account and let you create templates, manage contacts, and run campaigns.
  • To send WhatsApp messages on your behalf to the contacts you provide, using your own connected WhatsApp Business Account.
  • To display delivery, read, click, and reply analytics for your campaigns.
  • To enforce the limits and features included in your subscription plan.
  • To communicate with you about your account or support requests.

3. Sharing With Meta / WhatsApp

To provide our service, we send data to Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform (Graph API) on your behalf — for example, to create templates, register your phone number, and send messages. We do not sell your data or your contacts' data to third parties. Access tokens obtained through WhatsApp Embedded Signup are used solely to manage the WhatsApp Business Account you connected, and are stored securely and only accessible to your account.

4. Data Retention

We retain account, contact, template, campaign, and message data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us.

5. Your Choices

You can disconnect your WhatsApp Business Account, edit or delete your contacts, and delete your account at any time. Recipients of your campaigns can opt out of future messages, which we honor platform-wide for your account.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, contact us at info@dovix.ai or write to us at B-18, 2nd Floor, Shiv Marg, Bani Park, Jaipur, Rajasthan 302015, India.
